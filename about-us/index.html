ABOUT K TECHARA

The tech company with people at heart

Advance your business with K Techara. The trusted technology partner that listens and understands your needs to curate tailored solutions for your critical business outcomes.

At K Techara, we put people first in everything we do

We empower people to create sustainable value through the clever use of technology. We believe this is necessary, not just for sustainable social development, but also for the success of our customers.

K Techara is a leading technology provider for mid-market businesses

Our service portfolio is extensive. From Microsoft Azure expertise to multi-cloud strategies, private cloud implementations, and AI, we offer comprehensive cloud solutions.

We combine managed SOC services with extensive security software partnerships to provide advanced cybersecurity. While server management, networking, and datacentre solutions come under our complete infrastructure services. 

We help our customers achieve workplace transformation with modern collaboration tools and workspace solutions. And we enable procurement excellence with framework partnerships and streamlined processes.

It’s all part of how we advance your ambitions, supporting your ongoing transformation into a more productive business that can stay ahead in an agile word.

Over 1,500 partnerships and counting

We’re proud to work with over 1,500 partners, including Apple, Microsoft, HP, HPE, Dell, Cisco, Lenovo, and more.  

We’ve built strong relationships with our industry-leading technology partners, ensuring our specialists can help you maximise your technology investments at every stage.  

Our deep expertise has led to a long record of successfully delivering transformational IT services and solutions – as evidenced by our awards and accreditations.
