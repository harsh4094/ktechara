Identity and Access Management Services

Protect critical data with identity and access management solutions

Ways of working and how your people access your data and systems have transformed at an unprecedented rate. In the ever-changing threat landscape, cloud technologies can mitigate the effects of hybrid working on your security.

OVERVIEW

Securing identities in the hybrid workplace with IAM

Talk to an Expert
Identity and access management (IAM) is essential in the hybrid workplace. Whether you’re looking at an initial integration of Active Directory (AD) identities with Entra ID or you want to maximise your Entra ID Premium investment, our security experts can help.
Features

Critical data protected with the cloud

A secure, identity-driven environment should underpin any technology implementation to ensure that your sensitive information is protected while enabling your people to work smarter.

Our identity and access management security solutions ensure you have the control you require to secure your applications and networks to create a more productive and secure workforce.

Flexibility when roles and duties change

It can be difficult to manage digital identities with a growing number of applications being used across enterprises. With Entra ID, you can not only grant privileges but also revoke access or protect sensitive information if employee duties change.

Initiate, capture, manage and record digital identities

Ineffective IAM can pose a significant threat, not only to compliance but also your overall security. Avoid risking damage from both internal and external threats to sensitive information with better management of digital identities.

Identity integration

Our security experts will work with you to establish Microsoft 365 security requirements, whether that is Entra ID integration, or steps to achieving a synchronised identity model. 

  • Identity and Access Management disciplines and the Microsoft Cloud
  • Microsoft 365 Identity Model selection
  • Active Directory Domain Services
  • Identity Synchronisation with Entra ID Connect
  • Single Sign-on with Entra ID and Microsoft 365
  • Active Directory Federation Services

Entra ID Premium

Entra ID Premium helps you build on synchronisation and sign-on designs from your identity integration strategy to enhance security with stronger authentication, new approaches to authorisation, perimeter services and insights.

We’ll work with you to introduce Entra ID technologies, align them to your business problems, clarify priorities and set out a roadmap to support deeper design and implementation.
